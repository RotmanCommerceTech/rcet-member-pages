# About RCET

**Rotman Commerce Emerging Technologies** (RCET), Rotman Commerce, University of Toronto.
105 St. George St, Toronto, Ontario M5S 3E6 · rotmancommercetech@gmail.com ·
Instagram [@rotman.tech](https://www.instagram.com/rotman.tech/) ("Tech Leadership for a New Era")

Tagline on the old site: **Empowering With Technologies** — *To empower Rotman Commerce students to achieve more.*

## Mission

To empower Rotman Commerce students with cutting-edge research, industry exposure,
and experiential learning to create the number one business technology talent
pipeline in Canada.

## Vision

To leverage and expand Toronto's tech ecosystem and embrace our reputation as
Silicon Valley of the North — one of the largest and fastest-growing tech hubs in
the world.

## How it all started

> The mission is simple. We want to create the number one business technology
> talent pipeline in Canada. Not one of — the NUMBER ONE in Canada.
>
> We created RCET so when Rotman Commerce students are navigating their career
> choices, they are no longer confined to just 3 core streams around Finance,
> Consulting, and Accounting, but now, Tech.
>
> For the ones who want to work for the Microsoft's, Apple's and Google's of the
> world. For the ones who want to build and create, whether technical or
> non-technical. Those that will build startups and ventures that create tech
> innovation in Canada, and the well-versed investors and venture capitalists
> that will fund them.

Founded by **Marco Fuentes** (Founder & President; "A Message from the PVP" dated
1 September 2024) with **David Yeo** (Vice President).

## The five pillars of emerging technologies

| Pillar | Main focus | Description (from the site) |
|---|---|---|
| Data & Artificial Intelligence | Artificial Intelligence | Delves into the power of data and AI, exploring how analytics, machine learning and intelligent systems are driving efficiency, unlocking insights and transforming the way companies compete and grow. |
| Extended Reality | VR and the Metaverse | Explores how XR is reshaping customer experiences, training and collaboration across industries, and how businesses can harness its potential to innovate and engage. |
| Sustainable Technologies | Health and Sustainability | Highlights the role of tech in building a more responsible future — innovative technologies that reduce environmental impact and drive a greener future. |
| Computing & Connectivity | Cloud Computing | How enhanced connectivity and computational power are enabling smarter, faster and more integrated business solutions. |
| Information & Security | Cybersecurity | The protection and management of data in an increasingly digital landscape: cybersecurity, data privacy and risk management. |
