## Commerce & Connection — 11 October 2024
*Knowledge sharing · Partners: Meta, Professor Paolo Granata*

RCET's inaugural event, bringing together thought leaders, innovators and experts
from across the globe to explore the future of virtual reality and its impact on
various fields. Featured academic professors and researchers from top universities,
VR independent software vendors, and representatives from Meta.

- Panel with **Paolo Granata** — UofT professor, McLuhan Creativity & Technology
  Seminar, Book & Media Studies, founder of the Media Ethics Lab
- Panel with **Meta** — Meta's products, services, solution areas and the future of connection
- **VR reality panels** — Yale, Stanford and Columbia on their VR use cases and research
- **ISV discussions** — VR use cases across industries and what is to come
- **VR experiments** — students on Meta Quest headsets, networking with industry leaders

## AI Transformation Case Competition: Building AI Agents — 27 November 2024
*Case competition · Microsoft Canada Headquarters · Partners: Microsoft, BDO*

| Attendees | Finalists | Workshops |
|---|---|---|
| 65 | 5 | 2 |

The RCET × Microsoft AI Transformation Competition, held at Microsoft Canada, where
students use Microsoft technology to build custom AI agents with no coding skills
required. Students research and build use-cases for their AI solutions to drive an
end-customer through their digital transformation journey and solve a real-world
business problem, live at Microsoft Canada HQ. Included *AI Agents with Copilot
Studio*, interactive workshops and an info session.
