About RCET

The number one business-technology talent pipeline in Canada. That's the goal.

Rotman Commerce Emerging Technologies exists so that when Rotman Commerce students choose a career, they are no longer confined to three core streams. Finance, consulting, accounting, and now tech.

How it started

The mission is simple. We want to create the number one business technology talent pipeline in Canada. Not one of. The number one.

For the ones who want to work for the Microsofts, Apples and Googles of the world. For the ones who want to build and create, whether technical or non-technical. For the ones who will build the startups that create tech innovation in Canada, and the investors who will fund them.

Mission

Research, industry exposure, experiential learning

To empower Rotman Commerce students with cutting-edge research, industry exposure and experiential learning, and create the number one business-technology talent pipeline in Canada.

Vision

Silicon Valley of the North

To leverage and expand Toronto's tech ecosystem and embrace its reputation as one of the largest and fastest-growing tech hubs in the world.

Five pillars

The map we work from

Every event and research brief sits under one of these.

Data & Artificial Intelligence

Analytics, machine learning and intelligent systems that change how companies compete and grow.

Extended Reality

VR, AR and the metaverse reshaping customer experience, training and collaboration.

Sustainable Technologies

Clean tech, health tech and the innovations that reduce environmental impact.

Computing & Connectivity

Cloud computing and connectivity that make business smarter, faster and more integrated.

Information & Security

Cybersecurity, data privacy and risk management for a digital-first world.
